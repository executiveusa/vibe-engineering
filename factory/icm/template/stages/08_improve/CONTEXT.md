# Stage 08 — Improve

## Inputs

- Layer 4: `../07_ship/output/`.
- Real user feedback, analytics, support issues, costs, incidents, and commercial results.
- Original outcome and proof contract.

## Process

Compare reality with the promised outcome. Record learning, defects, adoption barriers, accessibility problems, cost, environmental impact where relevant, revenue or savings, and the smallest next improvement. Do not start a new workstream without replacing or parking another.

## Outputs

- `outcome-review.md` -> `output/`
- `learning-log.md` -> `output/`
- `next-slice.md` -> `output/`
- `plain-language-summary.md` -> `output/`

## Human gate

The human decides whether to SELL, USE, MERGE, PARK, or ARCHIVE the next work and which active workstream it replaces.

## Plain-language proof

Explain what people actually used, what helped, what failed, what it cost, and the single next action most likely to create a paid or verified outcome.
