# Stage 08 — Improve / Learn

One job: compare the promise with reality and choose the smallest better move.

## Inputs

- Layer 4: `../07_ship/output/`.
- Layer 4: `../00_intake/output/project-contract.md`.
- Real user feedback, analytics, support issues, costs, incidents, retention/churn reasons, and commercial results.
- Layer 3: `../../shared/VIBE_DECISION_STANDARD.md`.
- Layer 3: `../../shared/OWNERSHIP_RETENTION_STANDARD.md`.

## Process

1. Compare actual behavior with the original outcome and proof contract.
2. Record what people used, what helped, what failed, adoption barriers, accessibility problems, incidents, cost, environmental impact where relevant, and revenue/savings/validated learning.
3. Run the earned-retention loop: measure → show → explain → improve → teach → measure again. Record why users/customers stay, leave, or disengage without treating lock-in as success.
4. Check portability and ownership health: exports, account access, provider replacement, documentation, and handoff should remain usable as the system changes.
5. Identify the single smallest next improvement with Intent → Standard → Evidence. Do not turn learning into automatic feature growth.
6. Do not start a new workstream without replacing or parking another.

## Human gate

The human decides whether to SELL, USE, MERGE, PARK, or ARCHIVE the next work and which active workstream it replaces.

## Plain-language proof

Explain what people actually used, what helped, what failed, what it cost, why people stayed or left, whether the owner still controls the system, and the single next action most likely to create a paid or verified outcome.
