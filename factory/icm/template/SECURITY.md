## Non-negotiable rules

- Never commit, print, or paste secrets into project artifacts.
- Use least privilege and owner-controlled accounts.
- Treat health, identity, youth, senior, financial, location, and community data as sensitive.
- Record authentication, authorization, data retention, external services, abuse cases, and incident response during Blueprint and Verify.

## Reporting

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to the confirmed project owner. Do not publish exploitable details before a safe fix and disclosure decision.
