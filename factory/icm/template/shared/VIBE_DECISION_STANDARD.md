# Vibe decision standard

V.I.B.E. means **Verify It Before Everything**.

Use this reference when a stage is deciding what to do, what good means, or what counts as proof.

## Intent → Standard → Evidence

Every material decision answers:

1. **Intent** — What are we trying to change, for whom, and why?
2. **Standard** — What does good look like before the builder becomes attached to its answer?
3. **Evidence** — What observable result would make the claim true?

Confidence, effort, and model agreement are not evidence.

## Thinking habits

Use plain language first, then the technical term when useful:

- **Question the answer** — critical thinking.
- **See what connects** — systems thinking. Name dependencies, downstream effects, people affected, recurring cost, and what the decision makes normal.
- **Get back to what is true** — first-principles reasoning. Separate facts, constraints, assumptions, and inherited convention.
- **Build one piece and prove it** — disciplined incremental delivery.

## Divergence modes

Divergence is for exploration, not truth.

### STANDARD

Use one grounded path for factual work, approved implementation, verification, security conclusions, destructive actions, migrations, production release, and other reality-convergent work.

### DIVERGE

Use about five materially different candidates when alternatives add value: product shape, positioning, UX, creative direction, architecture options, debugging hypotheses, and similar open-ended work.

Verbalized Sampling may be used here to reduce model-default repetition. Treat verbalized probabilities as exploration signals only.

### FRONTIER

Use a wider tail search for a bounded experiment when conventional approaches have failed or the upside justifies novelty. Frontier candidates must stay reversible and isolated from hard safety/release decisions.

## Convergence rule

Build receives one approved slice. Do not keep redesigning while implementing. If new evidence breaks the selected path, return to the stage that owns the decision and update the artifact there.
