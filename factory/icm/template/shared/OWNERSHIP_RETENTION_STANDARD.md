# Ownership and earned-retention standard

A customer or owner should remain because the system keeps producing value, not because leaving is made artificially difficult.

## Ownership floor

Before release, the owner can answer:

- Where is the source code?
- Who controls the repository, domain, deployment, database, billing, and critical credentials?
- How is data exported in a useful format?
- How is the system moved to another provider or handed to another competent builder?
- What is the rollback path?

Unknown ownership is a release problem, not a paperwork problem.

## Earned retention loop

Use this after release:

`measure → show → explain → improve → teach → measure again`

Measure the outcome promised in Intake/Vision. Show the owner what changed. Explain the system plainly enough that they can make a decision. Improve the smallest high-value friction. Teach what the owner needs to operate or challenge the system. Measure again.

## What to record

- customer/user outcome;
- adoption or task completion;
- support burden;
- reliability/incidents;
- cost to operate;
- revenue, savings, or validated learning where relevant;
- accessibility or inclusion problems;
- portability/export health;
- why the customer is staying, leaving, or disengaging.

Do not count feature volume, model calls, agent activity, or time spent as customer value unless the agreed outcome depends on them.
