# Plain-language standard

Every major artifact must be understandable to a motivated teenager or older adult who has not studied software engineering.

## Required pattern

1. **What this is:** one direct sentence.
2. **Why it matters:** the real-world benefit or risk.
3. **Everyday analogy:** a respectful comparison to something familiar.
4. **Technical name:** the exact term professionals use.
5. **Proof:** what someone can see, run, or check.
6. **Owner action:** what the human must decide or do.

## Writing rules

- Define acronyms on first use.
- Prefer short paragraphs and concrete verbs.
- Do not hide uncertainty behind confident language.
- Do not use childish language or stereotypes.
- Describe health and science claims with evidence limits and approval requirements.
- Translate technical risk into human consequences: money, privacy, safety, time, access, trust, or environmental impact.
