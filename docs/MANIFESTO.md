# Vibe Engineering Manifesto

## One house. One method. Many agents.

Vibe Engineering is not a prompt collection and not a vendor-specific agent system.

The global method is:

`INTENT → SPEC → PLAN → BUILD → PROOF → COUNCIL → JUDGE → SHIP → OPERATE`

ICM is how that work stays interpretable, durable, and portable. The filesystem holds the truth. Agents are replaceable workers and adapters.

The canonical House Skill and `factory/icm/template/shared/VIBE_HOUSE_STANDARD.md` govern the current method. Older doctrine preserves history but does not create a competing lifecycle.

## Power without principles breeds chaos.

AI makes creation cheap. It does not make judgment automatic.

The danger is not that people without traditional engineering credentials can build. The danger is that speed can hide weak assumptions, inaccessible design, fragile architecture, unsafe data practices, false proof, and permanent platform dependence.

Vibe Engineering exists to preserve the freedom of AI-assisted creation while restoring discipline to the process.

## Verify It Before Everything.

The builder does not get to declare success because the code exists.

```text
BUILT ≠ VERIFIED
CI PASS ≠ CUSTOMER VALUE
DEPLOYED ≠ PRODUCTION PROOF
FORMAT PASS ≠ BEHAVIORAL PROOF
```

Proof must match the claim. Material work receives independent challenge. Judge returns only `SHIP` or `HOLD`.

## Freedom without ownership is just another dependency.

A customer should not need permission to access the thing they paid to create.

Our default position:

- the customer controls the agreed source code;
- the customer controls their data;
- the customer controls production accounts and credentials;
- the customer receives documentation, rollback, and export paths;
- vendors, models, and builders remain replaceable where reasonably possible;
- pricing reflects real value, human time, and compute rather than manufactured captivity.

Upstream owns the general Vibe method. Each project owns its specific facts and owner decisions.

## Engineering is a set of promises

Engineering is not a title reserved for people who memorize every implementation detail. It is a discipline of making promises visible and proving that they hold.

Those promises include:

- the product does what it says;
- risks are not hidden;
- failure is anticipated;
- recovery is possible;
- other people can understand and continue the work;
- the owner remains free.

## Heart & Soul is not decoration

Design is the deliberate shaping of human attention, effort, confidence, trust, emotion, accessibility, and control around a real outcome.

The Heart & Soul doctrine lives inside the same House lifecycle as engineering. It does not bypass proof or authority.

The subtraction rule is simple:

> Keep removing until another removal would harm user success, comprehension, trust, accessibility, control, emotional resonance, or essential identity.

Minimalism is optional. Essentiality is mandatory.

## Taste is judgment made visible

Taste is the ability to remove what does not belong, make the important obvious, respect people’s attention, and finish every visible decision.

Taste cannot excuse weak reliability or security. Reliability cannot excuse a confusing product. A polished interface cannot substitute for missing proof.

Vibe Engineering judges the whole system.

## The final measure

We do not measure success by how much an AI generated.

We measure how much useful, reliable, proven freedom the owner gained.
