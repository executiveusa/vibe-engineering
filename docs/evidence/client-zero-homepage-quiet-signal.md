# Client Zero Homepage — Quiet Signal

Issue: #32

## MODE
Brownfield / Client Zero.

## OUTCOME
Replace the homepage's acid-green AI-tech territory with a culturally current, restrained, high-class urban editorial direction while preserving clarity, accessibility, the Vibe doctrine, owner control, and the existing React/Vite architecture.

## TARGET
Young builders, creators, non-technical founders/operators, nonprofits, and culturally aware teams who want AI leverage without generic AI aesthetics.

## PRIMARY ACTION
Understand Vibe Engineering quickly and enter the method/source.

## BASELINE
The existing homepage was already short, accessible-minded, and coherent. Its main mismatch with owner direction was visual territory: near-black + acid green + cinematic AI/system montage read as explicit technology branding. The new brief asks for taste, restraint, cultural confidence, and a less literal AI identity.

## THREE TERRITORIES

### 1. Gallery Utility
Maximum editorial restraint, paper/ink, institutional clarity, quiet typography.

Strength: timeless and credible.
Risk: can feel too formal and culturally distant from younger builders.
Decision: do not select alone.

### 2. Street Editorial
Fashion/editorial rhythm, asymmetry, poster-scale typography, analog texture.

Strength: cultural energy and memorability.
Risk: can become performative or make the method look like a fashion brand.
Decision: do not select alone.

### 3. Quiet Signal — SELECTED
Gallery Utility discipline + Street Editorial rhythm, bounded by Vibe systems thinking.

Governing idea: **Software got cheap. Judgment did not.**

Visual behavior:
- warm ink and bone;
- oxide/burnt-clay signal color;
- publishing metadata instead of SaaS chrome;
- controlled asymmetry;
- typography and rules instead of card walls;
- subtle print/material texture;
- one quiet reveal language;
- no dependency on external media.

## SEVEN-LENS REVIEW

### 1. Human Outcome
Visitor should understand in under five seconds that Vibe Engineering is a disciplined way to build with AI while keeping judgment and control.

### 2. Information + Hierarchy
Primary message: `Software got cheap. Judgment didn't.`
Secondary: build with AI without surrendering taste, proof, or ownership.
Then: why now → method → beliefs → open system.

### 3. Interaction + Journey
One-page path with two primary choices: learn the method or inspect source. Existing optional soundtrack remains subordinate. No modal or complex navigation added.

### 4. Visual + Identity
Removed acid-green identity and procedural AI illustrations. Selected warm ink/paper/oxide palette and editorial metadata/grid behavior. No COLLINS or external visual motif copied.

### 5. Motion + Emotion
Only entrance/reveal motion with transform/opacity. Motion supports reading order. No autoplay audible media. Reduced-motion path remains.

### 6. System + Responsive + Accessibility
No new dependencies. Existing Vite/React/GSAP stack retained. Responsive rules explicitly cover 920px, 720px, 390px and min-width 320px. Focus-visible, skip link, reduced motion and increased contrast remain represented.

### 7. Trust + Proof + Sovereignty
Source link remains public. Copy emphasizes proof and ownership. No fake metrics, dashboards, operational state, or implied production claims added.

## SUBTRACTION GAUNTLET

### REMOVE
- cinematic app/automation/agent/system montage;
- acid-green primary CTA language;
- contrast cards comparing vibe coding vs Vibe Engineering;
- duplicate explanations of systems thinking;
- decorative UI simulations;
- separate hero button containers where text links communicate hierarchy better.

### MERGE
- previous `why` comparison and systems explanation into one `Why now` argument;
- public process and technical rhythm into one visible nine-verb flow;
- ownership/proof/design beliefs into four concise belief lines.

### INFER / DEFER
- technical ICM, Council, Judge, score, architecture and API details stay in the repository rather than the public homepage.

### KEEP
- V.I.B.E. meaning;
- owner-controlled soundtrack;
- public repository destination;
- systems thinking, taste, proof and ownership doctrine;
- accessibility/reduced-motion behavior.

### STOPPING POINT
Further removal would begin to damage either understanding of the method, trust in its rigor, or the owner's desired cultural identity.

## PROTOCOL ALIGNMENT
The design follows strategy before styling, distinction before decoration, self-evident navigation, content before containers, systems over isolated screens, and proof before claims.

## ACCEPTANCE
- no neon/acid green brand identity;
- no generic AI imagery or dashboard mockups;
- no default card/bento composition;
- primary message understandable without interaction;
- public source remains reachable;
- mobile remains intentionally composed;
- reduced motion remains supported;
- no new dependencies;
- CI/build/audit must pass before merge;
- independent taste review must name the largest remaining gap before merge.
