# Contagious Guerrilla Growth — Vibe Engineering

Ten original agent skills for read-only website growth audits, STEPPS-informed campaigns, ethical field activations and bounded measurement loops. Handcrafted for the Vibe House method; not copied from Jonah Berger's *Contagious: Why Things Catch On* (2013). Taleb-inspired experiment optionality does not guarantee positive outcomes.

## Use

`vibe skills` lists the new skill metadata; `vibe run contagious-campaign-director` loads its procedure. The skills do not execute campaigns without agent and human workflow.

## Required contract

MODE | OUTCOME | TARGET | OWNER | VERIFIED BASELINE | OFFER | ONE STEPPS MECHANISM | DESTINATION | PRIMARY ACTION | COST CAP | CONSENT | INDEPENDENT REVIEW | STOP | ROLLBACK | EVIDENCE.

## Authority

The existing `skills/vibe-engineering/SKILL.md` remains supreme. Audit before change. All automations default OFF. Explicit owner authorization per action for publishing, outreach, spending and account changes. Email execution for Digital Home clients prohibited. No manipulation, false testimonials, unverifiable results, unlicensed assets or intrusive tracking. Review independently before publishing. A merged skill or website page is not evidence of campaign success.
