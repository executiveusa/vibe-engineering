# Vibe Engineering Editorial Foundation

**Status:** Topic backlog only. No article is approved or published.  
**Audience:** young people, parents, educators, nontechnical creators, community organizations, and AI-assisted builders.

## Editorial promise

Every article must help the reader make, inspect, test, or own something. Generic AI summaries are rejected.

Every article must include:

- one plain-language idea;
- one real example;
- one activity or checklist;
- one verification step;
- one ownership or safety principle;
- one next action.

## Flagship article briefs

### The AI Slop Detector

**Reader problem:** The reader cannot tell whether AI output is useful or merely polished.  
**Original contribution:** A printable seven-question scorecard and before/after example.  
**Proof activity:** The reader scores one generated landing page and records unsupported claims, missing states, and absent evidence.  
**CTA:** Open the Workflow Locker’s Slop Detector process.

### Build One Verifiable Slice

**Reader problem:** The reader has a large idea and keeps generating disconnected code.  
**Original contribution:** A worksheet that converts an idea into one user, one action, one result, and one proof.  
**Proof activity:** Another person completes the action without verbal help.  
**CTA:** Start a Greenfield Slice workflow.

### Builders Cannot Approve Themselves

**Reader problem:** The same AI creates, tests, and declares its own work complete.  
**Original contribution:** A child-friendly courtroom analogy for Architect, Builder, Guardian, and Judge.  
**Proof activity:** Assign four people or agents different roles and compare findings.  
**CTA:** Run the Vibe Council.

## Content safety

- Do not claim that Vibe Engineering guarantees secure or correct software.
- Do not fabricate student outcomes, adoption numbers, customer results, awards, or testimonials.
- Clearly label estimates and opinions.
- Keep examples age-appropriate and avoid collecting children’s personal information.
- Human approval is required before publication.
