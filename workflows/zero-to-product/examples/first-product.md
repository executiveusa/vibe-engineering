# Example — first product from a non-technical founder

1. **Start:** install Vibe and run `vibe journey`.
2. **Discover:** `grill-idea` and `grill` turn “I want an app for my business” into a clear user, job, constraints, and standard.
3. **Define:** `map` and `spec` produce the smallest useful product contract and proof plan.
4. **Plan:** `tickets` creates small end-to-end slices.
5. **Build:** `build` executes one slice at a time; `test-first`, `debug`, and `deep-work` are used only where needed.
6. **Review:** `review` runs OpenCodeReview first, then `project-review`, `stop-slop`, and `taste` check the broader result.
7. **Prove:** `proof` ties claims to the exact candidate revision.
8. **Integrate:** `merge` combines approved work by intent.
9. **Release:** `ship` requires authorized SHIP, production smoke proof, and rollback.
10. **Learn:** `handoff` records what the next cycle should inherit.

The founder never needs to memorize the skill list. The agent exposes only the next decision and the next useful action.
