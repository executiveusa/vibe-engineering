# Contagious Campaign Director

Orchestrate ethical marketing experiments for an existing business.

## Procedure

1. Identify one buyer, offer and owned destination.
2. Audit current conversion before changing anything.
3. Select one lead STEPPS mechanism.
4. Rank three reversible experiments for customer value, cost and risk.
5. Obtain approval for publishing, outreach and spending.
6. Route implementation through Vibe House; measure conversion against baseline.

## Controls

Apply the canonical Vibe Engineering House Skill and project-local rules. Start read-only; separate verified evidence from assumptions. No spending, public publishing, account changes, outreach, contact capture, or automation without explicit per-action owner authorization. Digital Home clients prohibit email execution. The builder cannot approve itself. Independent review and rollback are mandatory before release. Never claim revenue, virality or production impact without live evidence.

## Source

Original procedure informed by Jonah Berger's STEPPS (Social Currency, Triggers, Emotion, Public, Practical Value, Stories); not a reproduction of the book.
